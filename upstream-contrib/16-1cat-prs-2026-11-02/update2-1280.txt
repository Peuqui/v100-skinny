Update 2026-11-02 (second): one commit added, dff18c253 "keep the fused SM75 prefill index scores in FP32". The fused Turing scorer of this PR rounded each head product q_h . k to fp16 before the ReLU. The index keys enter without their per-key scale (e4m3 values up to 448), so one product can pass fp16's 65,504, as #1327 describes for main's cuBLAS tile (#1328 fixes that one). The kernel now keeps the FP32 accumulator; registers and shared memory are unchanged, and two conversions per score fall away.

How large this is in a real model: on GLM-5.3-Flash, PP=5 over three Tesla V100 and two Quadro RTX 8000 with our fork's production entry, I counted the prefill index logits that feed the top-k, in the causal window, over prompts up to 516,196 tokens. Non-finite share per stage: 36.9 % and 31.0 % on the two RTX 8000 stages (this scorer), 24.3 %, 3.2 % and 53.8 % on the three V100 stages (the cuBLAS tile); single layers up to 70 %. With all three routes in FP32 (this commit, the Triton scorer of #1282 and the change of #1328), 0.000 % on every stage and every index layer over the same prompts. In an A/B of that fork build against the same build without the three changes (runs T T P T P, T#2 + T#4 against P#3 + P#5), time to first token at 516,196 tokens was 244.01 / 244.20 s against 248.42 / 248.32 s and prefill of 8,687 tokens 4.15 to 4.24 s against 4.21 to 4.30 s (leaving out the first request of P#3, the first boot of the reference build, at 6.00 s); short-prompt answers and GSM8K-30 (29/30) were identical, and one of the two reference runs ran into a repetition loop at 516,196 tokens while no fixed run did. DeepSeek-V4-Flash shows 0 % non-finite scores already without the change (same count, prompts up to 500,000 tokens); its query norms stay far below the overflow.
