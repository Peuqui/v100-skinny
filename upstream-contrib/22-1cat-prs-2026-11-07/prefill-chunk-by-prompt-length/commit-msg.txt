perf(scheduler): prefill short prompts in smaller chunks

With pipeline parallelism a prompt of few chunks spends much of its
prefill filling and draining the pipeline, while long prompts gain from
large chunks that read the weights less often per token. GLM-5.3-Flash
on PP=5 (RTX 8000 + V100): 1024-token chunks take 13.8 to 14 % less time
to the first token at 170k to 516k tokens but 41 % more at 1k and 3.7 %
more at 8.3k than 512-token chunks.

SchedulerConfig.short_prompt_chunk_tokens / short_prompt_threshold
(--short-prompt-chunk-tokens / --short-prompt-threshold, off by default):
a request whose uncached prompt part is shorter than the threshold when
it leaves the waiting queue is prefilled in chunks of that size, every
other one keeps max_num_batched_tokens. The decision is stored on the
request (prefill_chunk_cap), so a prefix-cache hit with a short new part
counts as a short prefill.

Test: tests/v1/core/test_short_prompt_chunks.py.
