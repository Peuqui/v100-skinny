## Ersetzte Abschnitte des PR-Textes

Neuer Titel:

[Feature][Qwen4Exp] PLE overflow cascade: device, pinned host, store cards, disk

In "Purpose", Stufe 3 ersetzen durch:

3. store cards, i.e. GPU memory the PLE offload worker fills once every
   pipeline stage has allocated its KV cache and captured its graphs; a card
   may be one that runs a stage,

Neuer Absatz am Ende von "Purpose":

Since the first version the store tier is a list of cards instead of one card
with a fixed budget. Under pipeline parallelism the other stages usually have
device memory left once the KV cache is allocated (the block count follows the
tightest stage), and the worker now places the table there. It reads each
card's free memory after the stages are set up, keeps a reserve free, and
fills the cards in order. With it, our PP4 rig runs Flash-Next with a 3 GiB
host share and nothing on disk, and keeps its fifth card free.

"Configuration" ersetzen durch:

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `VLLM_QWEN4EXP_PLE_STORE_DEVICES` | Visible CUDA indices of the store cards, comma-separated, in fill order. Setting it starts the cascade. |
| `VLLM_QWEN4EXP_PLE_STORE_RESERVE_GIB` | Memory each store card keeps free, one value per card. Default 0.5 GiB. Raise it for a card that other tenants share. |
| `VLLM_QWEN4EXP_PLE_DISK=1` | Let the remainder be read from the checkpoint. Also starts the cascade on its own (no store card). |
| `VLLM_QWEN4EXP_PLE_HOST_GIB` | Unchanged meaning: pinned host share per rank. |

Examples:

```
# PP4, the other pipeline stages hold the overflow
VLLM_QWEN4EXP_PLE_HOST_GIB=3 VLLM_QWEN4EXP_PLE_STORE_DEVICES=1,2,3 VLLM_QWEN4EXP_PLE_DISK=1 vllm serve ... --pipeline-parallel-size 4
# TP2 with a spare fifth card that also serves a vision model
VLLM_QWEN4EXP_PLE_HOST_GIB=2 VLLM_QWEN4EXP_PLE_STORE_DEVICES=4 VLLM_QWEN4EXP_PLE_STORE_RESERVE_GIB=10.3 vllm serve ...
```

Refused before any worker starts, in addition to the old checks: store
reserves without store devices or with a count that does not match them, and
store devices with `data_parallel_size` above 1 (the stages report their
claims within one replica).

In "How it works" ersetzen bzw. ergänzen:

- Placement: `plan_ple_placement` returns device, host and remote rows. How the
  worker splits the remote rows between its cards and the disk is decided
  later, in the worker (`plan_ple_worker_segments`), filling the cards in
  order and splitting a rank's range at a card boundary.
- Registration binds the ranks' placements but loads nothing: a store card may
  run a stage that has not allocated its KV cache yet.
- After `capture_model` and before the kernel warm-up (the first step that asks
  the worker for rows; under Model Runner V2 it runs real `execute_model`
  steps), every rank reports what its stage still claims on its card,
  `allocated + peak_activation - reserved` (the capture released the
  activations), through `all_gather_object` on the world group. The rank that
  spawned the worker sends the claims over the ready pipe, now duplex, and
  waits for the acknowledgement. The worker reads `mem_get_info` per card,
  subtracts claim and reserve, loads, and acknowledges; leftover rows go to
  disk or fail the start.
- The lookup gathers per card and writes only its own slots of the shared
  output buffer.
- The worker unmaps the checkpoint pages it read, after a disk-tier gather and
  after loading the store tier (`madvise(MADV_DONTNEED)` on the private,
  file-backed shard mappings; the pages stay in the page cache). Mapped pages
  are ones the kernel keeps: without this the worker held 11.2 GB of the
  checkpoint after loading a 25.8 GiB store tier, and its disk-tier pages grew
  by ~250 MiB per request while the kernel swapped other processes out. Only
  shards the loader recorded as file-backed are released; on anonymous memory
  `MADV_DONTNEED` discards data. The existing test
  `test_ngram_embedding_retains_and_gathers_disk_shards` stubbed that check
  with anonymous tensors and now loads a real safetensors file.
- `copy_ple_embedding_shard_` copies with `copy_` directly (see decision 1).

Neuer Abschnitt in "Test Result":

### Update 2026-09-24: store cards under PP4

`nvidia/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NVFP4`, TP1 x PP4 (12 layers per stage) on
2x Quadro RTX 8000 + 2x Tesla V100, MTP k=4, `--max-model-len 262144`.
12 sampled requests of ~29,200 tokens per row, after a discarded first request:

| Host share | Remote rows on | Decode tok/s, mean (range) | Prefill | MemAvailable | Swap-out per request |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 GiB | disk, 16.8 GiB | 36.0 (32.5–38.0) | 13.5–14.0 s | 2.7–3.3 GiB | every request |
| 12 GiB | stage 1 card, 16.8 GiB | 35.3 (32.8–37.6) | 13.5–13.6 s | 4.7–5.3 GiB | none |
| 3 GiB | spare fifth card, 25.8 GiB | 39.5 (32.7–45.8) | 13.5–13.6 s | 12 GiB | none |
| 3 GiB | stage 1 + 2 cards, 21.5 + 4.3 GiB | 36.9 (32.0–43.3) | 13.5–13.7 s | 13.7–14 GiB | none |

(The first row was measured before the unmapping fix.) Greedy output of the
last row is identical to the disk row; needles 4/4 at 24,488 and 101,605
tokens. A store card that also runs a stage served decode about 7 % slower
than a spare card; its gain is host memory and a free fifth card.

Disk tier with a cold page cache and real, varied text (six different
45,000-character journal slices, 15–17k tokens each; the word-list prompts
share most n-grams and hit the cache far more often): 47.7 GiB on disk, page
cache of the checkpoint 0.5–2.9 % resident, 155,000–200,000 major faults per
request. Prefill 10.0–12.8 s against 7.7–12.5 s with the table on the cards
(+0.3 to +2.5 s), decode unchanged.

Unmapping, same PP4 run with 12 GiB host share and 16.8 GiB on disk, twelve
different texts: worker RssFile 112 → 1,939 MiB and 2.1 GiB swapped out
before; 105–109 MiB and 96 MiB swapped out with the fix, prefill and decode
unchanged.

## Kommentar für den PR (nach dem Push)

Updated: the store tier is now a list of cards, placed by the offload worker
once every pipeline stage is set up, so under pipeline parallelism the other
stages' spare memory can hold the overflow. Two memory fixes came out of the
measurements: the worker no longer keeps the checkpoint pages it read mapped
(11.2 GB after a store load before), and shards are copied to the device
without a staging copy. The description has the new configuration, the PP4
numbers and a cold-cache disk measurement.
