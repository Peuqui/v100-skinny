[Perf][PP] Send decode and verify handovers as one packed message (kernel_config.pp_pack_handover)

Based on 1Cat main 73da3445d; three commits (the packed handover; its startup layout check and capture-time guard; the rejection of a setting it cannot honour).

Problem: At a pipeline stage boundary the intermediate tensors of a step are sent one message per tensor, and on a fixed-shape decode or verify step the receiving stage copies them into the buffers its CUDA graph reads. With GLM-5.3-Flash and the DFlash2 drafter on five stages a boundary carries 5 tensors (comb, post, residual, hidden_states and one auxiliary hidden state) at the first boundary and 8 at the last one (three more auxiliary hidden states), and a round crosses four boundaries. The sends are small (246,528 to 369,408 bytes at five rows).

Change:
- vllm/v1/worker/gpu/pp_pack.py (new): both sides derive the same byte layout from the tensors' names, dtypes and per-row shapes (segments sorted by name and aligned to 256 bytes) and exchange one flat byte block per handover. The sender's FULL CUDA graph writes its stage output into the packed block of its row count; the receiver receives into the block that the graph for that row count reads from, so neither side runs a pack or unpack copy outside the graphs. A step takes this path exactly when its batch descriptor is a FULL graph with a uniform token count (decode and verify); sender and receiver dispatch the same scheduler output, so they agree. Every other step, including prefill, keeps the per-tensor transfer unchanged.
- vllm/config/kernel.py: new KernelConfig field pp_pack_handover, default off, ignored in KernelConfig.compute_hash (the torch.compile artifacts are the same; the CUDA graphs of a stage that sends differ by the write into the packed block). Admission: pipeline parallel size above 1, tensor parallel size 1, no data parallelism, the V2 model runner, VLLM_V41_PP_RANK0 unset (that path has its own metadata-free transfer of the same boundary). A set field that misses one of these is a ValueError naming the condition when the Worker is constructed (third commit), instead of being ignored.
- tests/v1/worker/test_sm70_long_attention_graphs.py: its two fake engine configurations now carry kernel_config=KernelConfig(), which the graph manager reads.
- vllm/v1/worker/gpu/cudagraph_utils.py, vllm/v1/worker/gpu/model_runner.py, vllm/v1/worker/gpu_worker.py: the graph manager holds the packed send block and passes the receive block as the graph's intermediate-tensor input; the worker posts the packed receive after the runner's dispatch (the packed path depends on the batch descriptor) and sends the graph's own output block as one message; the per-tensor receive is factored into a method (_irecv_per_tensor_handover) so both paths share it.
- Safety (second commit): after graph capture every pipeline rank exchanges its packed send layout over the CPU group and the receiver raises on any difference (dtype, shape, graph sizes, or packed graphs on one side only) instead of hanging in NCCL. Allocating a packed block while a CUDA graph is being captured asserts.
- tests/v1/worker/test_pp_pack.py (new, 23 tests): layout and alignment, exact round trip of mixed dtypes, views into the flat block, admission and each rejected combination, a Worker built with an unsupported setting, the receive pool following the admission, graph inputs and send block for packed and unpacked steps, worker receive paths, one message across a group, refusal of block allocation during capture, layout handshake (match and mismatch).
- docs/design/architecture/runtime_reference.md: the generated compute_hash line number of KernelConfig moves with the new field.
- Additional costs: one packed send block per captured row count on a sending stage and one receive block per row count on a receiving stage (246,528 to 369,408 bytes each at five rows on GLM-5.3-Flash), allocated in the eager warmup before capture; one CPU-group exchange of the layouts per boundary at startup.
