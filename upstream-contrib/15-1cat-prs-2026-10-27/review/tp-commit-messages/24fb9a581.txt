fix(flash_v100): check the dense prefill entry without binding it

MLA prefill backend selection asks
flash_v100_dense_prefill_lse_available() while the model is built,
before the engine binds its Flash-V100 runtime. The check went through
get_flash_dense_forward(), which binds the operation and raised
"Flash-V100 resources must be bound before execution": the first
GLM-5.3-Flash boot failed with it. The loader is now separate from the
bound accessor; availability only loads, execution still binds.

Tests: tests/v1/attention/test_flash_v100_dense_prefill_availability.py,
2 passed (availability without a bound runtime; execution still
requires it).
