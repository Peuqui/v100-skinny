Title: [Bugfix][GLM-5.3] Compute the MoE router GEMM once per layer

Branch: pr-glm5next-router-once (base 1Cat main 022349e47), one commit, fork version 0ef69f4e6

----- Text -----

Problem: Glm5NextMoE builds its FusedMoE with gate=self.gate, so the MoE runner computes the router logits itself and discards the router_logits argument. Glm5NextMoE.forward nevertheless ran self.gate first and passed the result in. Every MoE layer of GLM-5.3-Flash therefore ran the router GEMM (and its cast to float32) twice per layer and step; one of the two results was never used.

Change (vllm/models/glm5next/nvidia/model.py): Glm5NextMoE.forward no longer calls self.gate. It asserts experts.is_internal_router and passes the hidden states as the placeholder router_logits argument, the way the DeepSeek-V2/V4 models do for an internal router. The comment that said main's MoERunner needs pre-computed logits is replaced; the runner computes them from the gate it was given.

Evidence:
- New unit test tests/models/glm5next/test_moe_router_once.py: a counting gate behind an experts module that mimics the runner (applies the gate itself, ignores router_logits). On main the gate runs 2 times per forward (assert 2 == 1 fails); with the change it runs once and the output equals the single gate result.
- The output cannot change: the removed computation was never consumed. In our fork, which carries the same change, the greedy answers of GLM-5.3-Flash to our eight control questions stayed token-identical across the update that brought it in.
- Speed: not measured for this change alone. It removes one router GEMM and its cast per MoE layer and step.
