[Perf][SM75] DeepSeek-V4 wo_a on Turing straight from block FP8: grouped QPN8 launch

- perf(qpn8): DeepSeek-V4 wo_a on Turing straight from FP8, grouped QPN8 launch
- perf(qpn8): grouped wo_a prefill as one batched product above M = 128

Problem: DeepSeek-V4's wo_a is a grouped matmul, 8 groups of [1024, 4096] block-FP8 rows per layer (o_groups 8, o_lora_rank 1024 in the model config). The Turing QPN8 block-FP8 kernel had no one-launch grouped mode, so on Turing process_weights_after_loading dequantized wo_a to an FP16 copy at load (67 MB per layer instead of 33.6 MB of FP8) and ran a cuBLAS einsum per call. On the two RTX stages of a mixed V100 + RTX 8000 pipeline this keeps the larger weight resident and reads twice the bytes in every decode step. Main does have a grouped QPN8 route in vllm/model_executor/kernels/linear/qpn/fp8.py, but for one layout only: wo_a under tensor parallel 4 with two groups of 1024 x 4096 per rank (2048 x 4096 together) in the serialized PP2 x TP4 single-request contract, run as a loop of one QPN8 launch per group. It has no one-launch grouped launch and no route for the 8-group layer without tensor parallelism that this PR serves.

Change (no new setting: wherever the QPN8 block-FP8 kernel is selected for wo_a, which is Turing, it now serves the layer):
- csrc/sm70_turbomind/ops/fp8_qpn8_sm70.cu: the packed QPN8 decode kernel takes a grouped launch (grid.y = group; codes [G, K, N], block scales [G, K/128, N/32], input [M, G times K], output [M, G times N]; M <= 8). fp8_qpn8_gemm_sm70_out and fp8_qpn8_prefill_sm70_out accept a leading group dimension on the codes and scales. Launches with grid.y = 1 are unchanged and the op signatures do not change.
- vllm/model_executor/kernels/linear/qpn/fp8_block.py: for a layer with is_bmm the one pack is split into per-group views (the pack is tile-major over 32 output rows, so a group's bytes are contiguous; the block scales are re-laid as one contiguous plane per group), a new custom op sm70_fp8::qpn8_native_grouped_linear runs the layer, and the layer has no direct-output route. M <= 8 is one launch over all groups. From 9 to 128 rows per group each group goes through the same M ladder as the dense QPN8 linears (Turing tensor-core GEMM). Above 128 rows per group the whole weight is dequantized into the dense prefill buffer (_block_qpn8_prefill_dense of #1535: the shared prefill workspace when CUDA graphs capture the prefill or the KV budget is fixed, otherwise a buffer for the call) and one at::bmm_out runs over the groups. wo_a has as many elements as wq_b and wo_b (33.55 million each for DeepSeek-V4-Flash), so the shared workspace does not grow.
- vllm/model_executor/layers/quantization/fp8.py: the branch that dequantized a grouped layer to FP16 when the QPN8 kernel had been selected on Turing is removed; Marlin-backed layers keep their dequantizing path. Layers served by the TurboMind grouped route (the V100 stages in our setup) do not take this path.
- tests: tests/quantization/test_fp8_marlin_bmm_dequant.py loses test_late_grouped_turing_metadata_discards_dense_qpn8_selection, which pinned the old FP16 route; the grouped route is covered by the new tests/quantization/test_qpn8_grouped_bmm.py (host-side logic with recorded native ops) and tests/kernels/quantization/test_sm70_qpn8_grouped_bmm.py (numerics on a GPU, including M = 129..1024 against the old einsum). benchmarks/kernels/benchmark_dsv4_wo_a_grouped.py compares the arms.
