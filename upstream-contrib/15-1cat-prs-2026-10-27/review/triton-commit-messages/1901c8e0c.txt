perf(dsv4): Triton prefill index scorer for the SM70-family relu route

With kernel_config.triton_sm7x_tensor_cores the indexer prefill scores
come from one Triton kernel instead of the cuBLAS route, on Volta and on
Turing chunks that the fused SM75 scorer does not take. Turing keeps
that native scorer for 32 and 64 heads: summed over long contexts it is
faster there (below). The kernel stacks the heads of BLOCK_T tokens into
one MMA operand that every key tile reuses, widens e4m3 keys bit-exactly
to fp16 (value * 2^-8), rounds the products to fp16 as the cuBLAS route
stores them, and keeps relu, head weights and head sum in registers. It
replaces the per-head FMA kernel _contiguous_index_logits_relu_kernel,
which indexer_prefill_cublas=False reaches without tensor-core Triton
(now with an FMA-sized tile).

SparseAttnIndexer and SparseAttnIndexerKpool decide the route once when
they are built (KernelConfig.use_triton_sm7x_tensor_cores) and pass it
to their custom op.

Tiles are measured per card: a sweep of 216 tiles over 512-token chunks
with 4.8k to 76.8k keys (76.8k = 307k context compressed 1:4). Turing
takes 2048 score elements per warp for 64 and 32 heads alike; Volta's
two model head counts keep their own tile.

CUDA graphs, sum over 4.8k/19.2k/38.4k/57.6k/76.8k keys, ms (less is
better), the route without the setting -> Triton:
  V100      64 heads 46.94 -> 28.87 (-38.5 %), 32 heads 23.96 -> 17.18 (-28.3 %)
  RTX 8000  64 heads 23.97 -> 24.21 (+1.0 %),  32 heads 12.53 -> 13.12 (+4.7 %)
On Turing Triton is faster up to 19.2k keys and 2-7 % slower beyond,
hence the fused scorer stays there. Max error against the fp64
reference equals the cuBLAS route on each card (1.33e-4 RTX, 2.34e-4
V100, relative to the largest score).

Tests (tensor-core cases need a Triton that emits MMA):
tests/kernels/test_deepseek_v4_sm70_indexer.py with the sm7x Triton: 39
passed (RTX 8000), 38 passed 1 skipped (V100); with stock Triton 31
passed 8 skipped (RTX 8000). A new case checks per card and head count
that the setting keeps the fused Turing scorer for 32 and 64 heads and
never takes the cuBLAS route.
