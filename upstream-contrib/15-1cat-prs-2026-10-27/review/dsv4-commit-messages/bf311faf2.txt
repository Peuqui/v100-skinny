perf(dsv4): gather the compressed KV cache with 2048 workers

Every prefill chunk dequantizes its request's whole compressed cache
(18.75k rows on average over a 150k prefill at ratio 4, 37.5k at its
end) into the workspace the sparse attention gathers from. The kernel
gave each request 128 workers that walk their rows one by one, which
left most SMs idle. 2048 two-warp workers keep the kernel and its
arithmetic and only spread the rows; the output is bit-identical.

CUDA graphs, us per call (less is better), 128 x 4 warps -> 2048 x 2:
  rows          2048    18750    37500
  RTX 8000  47.2 -> 7.2   532.3 -> 107.3   1061.9 -> 215.6
  V100      45.8 -> 7.7   616.4 ->  87.4   1229.3 -> 167.7
The DSv4 150k-prefill profile spends 0.76 s per RTX stage and 0.82 s
per V100 stage in this kernel.

The Triton gather also serves the ROCm backend and NVIDIA GPUs without
CuteDSL (dequantize_and_gather_k_cache); only V100 and RTX 8000 were
measured.

Tests: tests/kernels/test_deepseek_v4_sm70_qnorm_rope_kv_insert.py and
tests/kernels/test_compressor_kv_cache.py on an RTX 8000 and a V100, 2
and 32 passed (6 skipped) each; the microbenchmark above compared every
grid bit for bit with the 128-worker launch.
