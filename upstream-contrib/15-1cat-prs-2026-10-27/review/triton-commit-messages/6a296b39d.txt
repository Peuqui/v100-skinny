perf(dsv4): sparse prefill matmuls read their rows on tensor cores

With kernel_config.triton_sm7x_tensor_cores the DSv4 sparse MLA prefill
no longer gathers every token's selected rows into a [T, W, 512]
workspace that two cuBLAS BMMs then read twice. A scores GEMM and a PV
GEMM read the selected kv rows through the indices themselves, each
program covering all heads of its token (cuBLAS ran these M=64 batches
in 128-row tiles). The sink softmax kernel is shared; FP32 scores and
FP16 probabilities into PV are kept. One tile pair serves V100 and
RTX 8000. Fewer than 16 heads are padded for tl.dot and masked.

Whole 508-token chunk, CUDA graphs, ms per call (less is better),
today's route -> tensor cores:
  width 640 random indices   RTX 8000 3.064 -> 1.733  V100 2.452 -> 1.352
  width 640 shared indices            2.632 -> 1.228       2.206 -> 1.104
  width 1152 random indices           5.232 -> 3.047       4.287 -> 2.379
The workspace per pass shrinks from 110 to 30 MiB (width 640) and from
198 to 54 MiB (width 1152). The DSv4 150k-prefill profile spends 8.3 s
per RTX stage and 6.9 s per V100 stage in gather, BMMs and softmax.

Outputs: bit-identical to today's route on V100; on RTX 8000 0.97-1.25 %
of the FP16 outputs differ by one step, and the error against an fp64
reference equals today's route (max 4.87e-4 / mean 2.57e-4 relative,
128 tokens, width 640).

Tests: tests/kernels/attention/test_dsv4_sparse_prefill_bmm.py runs both
routes against the fp64 reference (8/64 heads, width 128/640, stale
tails, 300 tokens in three passes, FP32 score range): 34 passed on RTX
8000 and V100 with the sm7x Triton, 17 passed 17 skipped with stock
Triton. pre-commit and mypy-3.10 pass.
