perf(dsv4): attend a whole prefill chunk in one sparse pass

The tensor-core sparse MLA prefill attended 128 query tokens per pass,
a bound sized for the gather route's key buffer. Its own buffers are
only FP32 scores and FP16 probabilities, and one pass per 1024-token
chunk uses the GPU better, with neighbouring tokens selecting mostly
the same keys (DeepSeek-V4 top-k plus window). The route now takes up
to 1024 tokens per pass on every card; the gather route keeps 128. No
swept tile beat today's on either card with shared indices (a sweep on
random indices pointed to a different scores tile that loses on shared
ones), so the tiles stay constants.

Whole 1020-token chunk, 64 heads, CUDA graph (ms, less is better),
128-token passes -> one pass: RTX 8000 shared indices 640 slots
2.448 -> 2.194, 1152 slots 4.209 -> 3.859, random 3.629 -> 3.270,
6.085 -> 5.926; V100 640 slots 2.199 -> 1.919. Outputs bit-identical.
The pass buffers grow to 432 MiB at 1152 slots (378 MiB more).

DeepSeek-V4-Flash PP=5 (three V100, two RTX 8000), fork production with
kernel_config.triton_sm7x_tensor_cores against the same plus this
change and the fused Turing indexer scorer of the scorer commit,
A/B/A/B, s (less is better): prefill of 178,480 tokens 41.40 / 41.34 ->
41.18 / 41.17, of 287,039 tokens 74.04 / 74.02 -> 73.45 / 73.43;
GSM8K-30 29 correct and the eight quality answers byte-identical in
every boot. Peak memory.used of the V100 stages 32,048-32,108 ->
32,488 MiB with the KV cache unchanged at 317,261 tokens.

Tests: tests/kernels/attention/test_dsv4_sparse_prefill_bmm.py 35 passed
on the RTX 8000 and the V100 with the sm7x Triton, 17 passed 18 skipped
with stock Triton (new: the tensor-core route over two full passes and a
partial one against the BLAS route); test_deepseek_v4_sm70_routes.py 30
passed with each.
