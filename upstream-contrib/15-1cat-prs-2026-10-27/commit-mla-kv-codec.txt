[Bugfix][KV] MLA specs carry the codec of their cache dtype

Since #1251 the KV allocators of both runners reshape every carrier with
the backend shape for AttentionSpec.layout_cache_dtype(cache_dtype), which
falls back to "auto" when the spec's kv_quant_mode does not match the
configured dtype. MLAAttention.get_kv_cache_spec, the DeepSeek-V4
compressed attention, its sliding-window cache (DeepseekV4SWACache),
SlidingWindowMLASpec.merge and the sliding-window-to-full conversion in
kv_cache_utils build their MLA specs with cache_dtype_str only, so
kv_quant_mode stays NONE for packed fp8 pages:

- GLM-5.3 with fp8_e4m3 KV: the page holds 520-byte token rows, the
  "auto" shape has 512, and the reshape fails at boot.
- DeepSeek-V4 sliding-window cache (fp8_ds_mla): 584-byte rows inside a
  576-aligned page; the "auto" shape of 512 still fits, so the reshape
  succeeds as a strided view and the SM70 sparse decode leaves its BMM
  path for the paged fallback.

MLAAttentionSpec and SlidingWindowMLASpec now derive kv_quant_mode from
cache_dtype_str in __post_init__ and reject a contradicting mode, so every
construction site, including merges, gets the codec without repeating it.

The padding-aware copy_with_new_block_size from #1256 moves from
KVCacheSpec to AttentionSpec: only attention specs have a padded page
whose payload grows with the block size, and on KVCacheSpec the method
reads fields the base class does not declare (mypy attr-defined and
call-arg).
