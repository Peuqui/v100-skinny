## Purpose

DeepSeek-V4 with its DSpark drafter runs only on Model Runner V1: MRV2 admits the speculative method 'dspark' only for an Inkling 2 drafter (main's #1616) and rejects it for every other. This PR adds it for the other drafters, in three commits.

1. A DSpark speculator for MRV2, ported from vllm-project/vllm's MRV2 DSparkSpeculator (vllm#46995 by benchislett, speculator as of 2026-09-30) onto this fork's V2 DFlash speculator. The anchor is the first query position (SAMPLE_FROM_ANCHOR), the sequential Markov sampling runs inside the drafter's FULL CUDA graph, the DeepSeek-V4 context slots are passed per cache layer, the staging buffers are sized to the drafter's hidden size instead of the hc_mult-wide DeepSeek-V4 MTP residual, and the drafter keeps its own embedding copy under pipeline parallelism, the same contract as the V1 proposer. MRV2 admits 'dspark' in general (the two Inkling 2 exceptions that #1616 added to _validate_v2_model_runner become redundant and go; init_speculator keeps an Inkling 2 drafter on main's Inkling2DSparkSpeculator and gives every other DSpark drafter this speculator, which lives in dspark/block_drafter.py next to main's speculator.py), reads dspark_target_layer_ids and dspark_noise_token_id, and feeds the drafter the target hidden state unless the target returns an override (DeepSeek-V4 MTP only); the two inline call sites of that override now share one helper that also accepts a missing override. Credit: Co-authored-by Benjamin Chislett on the commit. Not ported: the speculators-format 1+N layout, the reduced draft vocabulary scatter, the top-k Markov projection, the confidence head and confidence-scheduled verification (rejected on MRV2 with a pointer to V1), acceptance estimation and watermarking.
2. Compressed-index decode graphs at context buckets for MRV2. MRV2 captured the DeepSeek-V4 decode graph at the maximum model length, where the SM70 indexer stays on its paged kernel; a 35.7k-token decode step took 91-92 ms. The graph manager now captures variants at the compressed-index buckets of the SM70 graph plan, the plan the V1 dispatcher already reads, builds the capture metadata at the bucket, and replays the smallest captured bucket that covers the live context. As in the V1 dispatcher, the variants exist for one request with one verification block. main's _add_dspark_width_graphs (#1616: target graphs for narrower verify widths) stays with the Inkling 2 drafter, the only one whose draft width shrinks with the batch; for this speculator it would capture graphs that are never replayed.
3. Tests for both, and for the width-graph condition.

The layering baseline records the one SM70 graph-plan import of the MRV2 graph manager (cudagraph_utils.py, platform 30 -> 31) and the new speculator's references to the DFlash base it extends (block_drafter.py), as it does for vllm/v1/spec_decode/dspark.py.
