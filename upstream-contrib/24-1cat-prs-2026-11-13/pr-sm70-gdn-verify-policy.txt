[Bugfix][SM70] Run the fused GDN verify projection under the engine's FP8 policy

Problem: fp8_qpn8_dispatch_ba_masked_sm70_out (b68d40f88) is registered in csrc/torch_bindings.cpp with a native_policy argument, but it is missing from CONFIGURED_OPERATORS in vllm/_sm70/policy.py and has no Python wrapper or fake next to its sibling fp8_qpn8_dispatch_ba_split_sm70_out. tests/quantization/test_sm70_native_policy.py::test_packaged_native_policy_schemas_parse_before_build fails on main for that. Its only caller, apply_gdn_ba_verify in vllm/model_executor/layers/quantization/sm70_gdn_ba_verify.py, calls the masked and the split operator straight through torch.ops._C without native_policy. With no token the operator opens no PolicyScope, so the fused verify projection runs with the native defaults instead of the engine's captured FP8 linear policy, under which the QKVZ projection it replaces runs. This matters whenever the engine's FP8 linear policy differs from the defaults.

Change:
1. The masked operator joins CONFIGURED_OPERATORS, gets a wrapper and a fake next to the split operator and is exported from vllm._sm70_ops.
2. sm70_gdn_ba_verify.py registers the opaque policy op sm70_gdn_ba_verify_dispatch with register_policy_op, as the channel-FP8 QPN8 dispatch of the compressed-tensors W8A16 scheme does. It picks the masked operator below eight rows and the split operator from eight rows on, and passes no dense workspace: the verify rows (2 to 32 at K = 5120) never take the dense large-M route that reads it.
3. bind_gdn_ba_verify_policy(vllm_config) captures the "fp8" linear policy while the layer is built (None with LoRA, off SM70 or without the operators). It replaces the enable_sm70_gdn_ba_verify flag, and apply_gdn_ba_verify passes the bound token. Binding by layer prefix, as the regular BA dispatch does, would not cover this path: the verify fusion also serves channel-FP8 layers that register no layer workspace.
4. qwen_gdn_linear_attn.py imports the verify module once under a neutral alias instead of four local imports. Tests that patch apply_gdn_ba_verify on the module still reach the patched function, and the file's platform coupling count in tools/pre_commit/layering_baseline.json drops from 407 to 404.

Not a duplicate: no open PR touches sm70_gdn_ba_verify.py, test_sm70_gdn_ba_verify.py or the masked operator. Open PRs that touch the same files do so for their own features (vllm/_sm70/linear.py, policy.py and _sm70_ops.py: #1278, #1282, #1286, #1347, #1348; _sm70_ops.py: #822, #890, #1092, #1125; layering_baseline.json: #1221, #1379). #983 adds a line next to the enable_sm70_gdn_ba_verify flag this PR replaces; if it merges first, we rebase.
