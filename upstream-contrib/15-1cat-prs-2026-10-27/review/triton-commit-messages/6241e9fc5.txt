feat(triton): one setting for tensor-core Triton on Volta and Turing

Stock Triton lowers tl.dot to FMA before SM80, so the SM70-family routes
use cuBLAS or hand-written kernels on Volta and Turing. A Triton built
with the sm_70 (mma.m8n8k4) and sm_75 (mma.m16n8k8) lowering makes
Triton kernels competitive there.

kernel_config.triton_sm7x_tensor_cores (default off) states that such a
Triton is installed; the field is part of the compilation hash.
KernelConfig.use_triton_sm7x_tensor_cores(device_index=None) is the one
place routes ask. It answers for the device's own architecture and, on
first use per architecture, compiles a 16x16 fp16 tl.dot without
launching it and requires mma.sync in the PTX (vllm/triton_utils/
mma_probe.py, generic). With the setting on and a Triton that lowers
tl.dot to FMA there, the engine stops with the compiler's path instead
of running FMA kernels.

Checked on this machine: stock Triton 3.6 raises on the RTX 8000 and the
V100, the sm7x build passes on both. Tests (no GPU needed):
tests/kernels/test_kernel_config_triton_sm7x.py, 8 passed with each
Triton.
