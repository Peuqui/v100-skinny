perf(dsv4): fused lightning-indexer prefill scores on Turing tensor cores

The SM70-family relu route scores prefill chunks by stacking the heads into
M for one cuBLAS GEMM, writing the [tokens * heads, keys] fp16 scores (64 MiB
per 512-token chunk and 1024 keys) and reading them back in a second kernel
for relu, head weights, head sum and key scales. On the DSv4 RTX stages that
pair took 6.9 s of a 51 s 150k prefill (nsys, cuBLAS 4.07 s + head sum 2.80 s),
and the RTX stages bound the pipeline (V100 stages 47-48 s).

indexer_prefill_scores_sm75 does it in one pass: each warp holds one token's
queries for 32 heads as m16n8k8 A fragments in registers, a CTA stages e4m3
key tiles widened to fp16 in shared memory (double buffered), scores are
rounded to fp16 as the cuBLAS route stores them, and relu, head weights and
the head sum stay in registers with a shuffle reduce-scatter; only the
[tokens, keys] result reaches memory. 32 or 64 heads (GLM pads 16 to 32).

Microbench (RTX 8000, CUDA graphs, 512 tokens x 64 heads): 1024 keys
0.326 -> 0.151 ms, 4096 keys 1.270 -> 0.586 ms, 18750 keys 5.551 -> 2.305 ms,
37500 keys 11.018 -> 4.518 ms (57-70 TFLOPS); max error against fp64 equal
to the cuBLAS route in all shapes, 32-51 % of values bit-identical (head-sum
order).

The chunk takes it when the indexer runs the relu route on an SM75
device (the query's own) with 32 or 64 heads; other chunks keep the
cuBLAS or per-head Triton route, and indexer_prefill_cublas decides only
between those two. The source builds for sm_75 only, and the op checks
the device, dtypes, strides and alignment it relies on. Each route logs
its choice once per device.

Tests: tests/kernels/test_deepseek_v4_sm70_indexer.py, 31 passed on an
RTX 8000; 30 passed and 1 skipped (the fused-route case needs SM75) on a
V100. The model-head-count cases (32 and 64 heads, 37 queries, 1100
keys) compare with an fp64 reference that rounds the per-head scores to
fp16 as every prefill route does.
