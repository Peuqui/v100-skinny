[Bugfix][SM70] Hold the block-FP8 prefill workspace only when graphs capture the prefill or the KV budget is fixed

Problem: 280b02436 replaced the per-call FP16 weight buffer of the block-FP8 QPN8 dense prefill (fp8_block.py, _qpn8_native_linear_write and the gated twin) with _ensure_block_qpn8_prefill_workspace, a buffer of the largest K*N that lives as long as the engine. The purpose, as test_block_qpn8_row_blocks_and_cudagraph_reuse_prefill_workspace shows, is that CUDA graphs of prefill-sized batches share one buffer instead of every captured layer taking its own K*N from the graph pool. process_weights_after_loading sets the workspace up for every engine, though, whatever it captures. In an engine whose graphs stop at a few decode rows the dense prefill always runs eagerly; the per-call buffer used to come from the activation room the startup profile reserves anyway, the resident one comes out of the KV cache.

Seen on GLM-5.3-Flash NVFP4 (8-bit QPN8 projections, ModelOptFp8PbWoLinearMethod) in PP5 on 3x V100 32 GB + 2x Quadro RTX 8000, cudagraph_capture_sizes [8]: per stage 128 MiB more in use after the merge of main 4f565bae3 (288 MiB on the last stage), the KV pool fell from 550,502 to 511,180 tokens, and a 516k-token prompt that ran before no longer finished. torch's allocation history on both builds (torch.cuda.memory._record_memory_history, snapshot after load) puts the whole difference per stage in fp8_block.py _ensure_block_qpn8_prefill_workspace; the KV allocation is smaller by the same amount and nothing else differs by more than 8 MiB.

Change (vllm/model_executor/kernels/linear/qpn/fp8_block.py):
1. _graphs_capture_dense_prefill(dense_above): the workspace is needed for the engine's life when CUDA graphs capture batches of more than dense_above rows (cudagraph_mode not NONE and a capture size above it), or when cache_config.kv_cache_memory_bytes is set, because a fixed KV budget skips the peak-memory deduction (the same reason #1320 keeps resident QPN4 scratch for fixed budgets). Without an engine config it keeps today's behaviour.
2. process_weights_after_loading sets the workspace up only in that case, with dense_above = 8 (_QPN8_MAX_M, the largest batch every layer serves without the dense prefill).
3. The two prefill sites take the buffer through _block_qpn8_prefill_dense: the shared workspace while a graph is captured or once one exists, otherwise a buffer for the call, as before 280b02436. The capture and per-stream behaviour of _ensure_block_qpn8_prefill_workspace is unchanged.
4. New test test_prefill_workspace_is_held_only_for_captured_prefill in tests/kernels/quantization/test_sm70_qpn8_block_fp8.py: capture sizes [8] (no workspace after load and after an eager 1024-row prefill), [8, 1024] (workspace after load), [8] with kv_cache_memory_bytes set (workspace after load); every case checks the 1024-row output against the dequantized reference.

Speed: the eager prefill allocates from the caching allocator, which costs microseconds against the dequantization of a K*N weight. In our A/B on the fork (GLM, T with this change against T without and our previous production), 8.7k-token prefill 3.86 to 3.89 s in every arm, decode and long-context step times within noise, KV pool back at 550,502 tokens and the 516k prompt runs again (TTFT 197.76 s, before the regression 198.02 s).

Not a duplicate: of the 58 open PRs, six change fp8_block.py (#1278, #1282, #1286, #1347, #1348 from us, the draft queue #1448); none touches the prefill workspace or its call sites (checked their diffs). Title searches for prefill workspace, qpn8 workspace, _block_qpn8_prefill, fp8_block and KV cache memory workspace find nothing else. #1320 made the same change for QPN4 scratch with enforce_eager as the trigger; here the trigger is the captured size, because an engine can capture decode graphs and still run every prefill eagerly.

Tests (pre-commit ruff-check, ruff-format and mypy-3.12 pass on the changed files), on main 4f565bae3 with this change:
- test_sm70_qpn8_block_fp8.py: 152 passed on a V100, 135 passed and 17 skipped on a Quadro RTX 8000. The new test on main without this change: the case with capture sizes [8] fails on both cards (the workspace is there after load), the other two pass.
- On both cards, all passing (skips are the SM70-only cases on the RTX 8000): test_sm70_fp8_prefill_exact_dense.py (24), test_sm70_fp8_workspace_aot_reload.py (7), test_sm70_native_policy.py (91), test_block_fp8_qpn8_capabilities.py (24), test_sm70_fp8_block32_route.py (V100 9), test_sm70_fp8_block32.py (V100 29), test_sm70_quantized_batch_supply.py (V100 100), glm5next/test_sm70_sparse.py (V100 10, RTX 8000 4 passed, 6 skipped).
- Our fork carries the same change (plus the SM75 tensor-core branch of #1278, which gets the same condition with its own bound of 512 rows); its capture-size condition runs in our production (GLM-5.3, DeepSeek-V4, Qwen3.8-27B FP8) since 16.11.2026, the fixed-budget condition followed the same day (none of these entries sets a fixed budget).
