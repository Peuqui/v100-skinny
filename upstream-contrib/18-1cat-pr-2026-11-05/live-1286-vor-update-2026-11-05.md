## Purpose

Serve NVIDIA's GLM-5.3-Flash-FP4-Local checkpoint (ModelOpt MIXED_PRECISION: NVFP4 routed experts with csf_32x2_v1 compressed block scales, MXFP8 KDA and dense projections, block-FP8 MLA projections) on SM70 and SM75 without converting the checkpoint. Unpacked to E4M3 the expert scales add 8.1 GiB and the model no longer fits five 32/48 GiB cards, so the scales stay compressed on the GPU, and the 8-bit projections stay 8-bit in QPN8 instead of being widened to fp16 at load. Our deployment: PP=5 over three Tesla V100 and two Quadro RTX 8000 with DFlash2, 524,288 tokens of context (docs/design/sm70_glm53_pp5_dflash2_mixed.md, added here).

Stacked PR. The branch sits on #1282 (Triton routes, itself on #1280, #1279, #1278 and #1216; #1222 and #1227 below them are merged). #1274 and #1275, which the boot needs, are merged as well. Then the GLM commits. Please review from c05d939cc on (git log c05d939cc~1..814c88909).

Groups of the GLM commits:

1. Loading and correctness (d6c8b9e0d, d225cc318, 43567bd4e, 64277a325, a6c286da2): csf_32x2_v1 decoder (with an encoder whose output matches the reference implementation in TensorFold byte for byte) and an expander that fills a shared QPN scale raster with the routed experts before each grouped skinny call (fixed launch shape, CUDA-graph safe; experts opt in through the CsfScaleConsumer protocol, other experts are rejected when the method is built); ModelOpt MIXED_PRECISION detection of weight_scale_encoding and a CSF loader for the expert scales; FP8_PB_WO linears through w8a8_block_fp8_linear (in modelopt.py, so for every architecture); block scales held next to their weights across AutoWeightsLoader calls (before, a scale that preceded its FP8 weight in the shards was silently dropped, the MLA projections stayed zero and the output was garbled); csf32 parts routed by their checkpoint name; the indexer query built in fp16 on Turing like the indexer op; Turing admitted on the FP16 sparse-MLA, KPool, indexer, mHC and KDA paths, each gate reading the capability of the worker's own device. Three GLM/ModelOpt test files that failed on main as well follow the current code.
2. Sparse MLA prefill with FP8 KV on cuBLAS blocks (e9e65989c): the Triton kernel (tl.dot as FMA before SM80, 78-87 % of the prefill compute) is replaced by the decode's gather/dequantize + cuBLAS GEMM route in blocks of 16 tokens. In an nsys profile of a 14,038-token prefill this part took 68.72 s before and 10.75 s after.
3. 8-bit projections in QPN8 (451b7c54a, 7a3bc12fb, 687de2508, 43db66d52): a third QPN8 scale mode [K/32, N] for MXFP8 (prepare, dequantization bit-identical to load-time unpacking, GEMV for M <= 8; accepts E8M0 exponents 95..134, which are exact in fp16); MXFP8 KDA q/k/v, KDA o_proj and the dense MLPs kept as MXFP8 linears (the KDA layer keeps its quant config and splits in_proj into a quantized qkv and a BF16 b/f_a/g_a part; all KDA layers of a pipeline stage must agree, a mixed checkpoint stops at load with an error); a Turing m16n8k8 path for the row-group scales at M 9..512; MLA q_b_proj, kv_b_proj and o_proj kept as block FP8 when the checkpoint names them. The MXFP8 kernel choice now sees the layer's shape (693efc243): Mxfp8LinearLayerConfig carries the partition's (N, K), the ModelOpt, online and compressed-tensors MXFP8 methods choose their kernel in create_weights, and the QPN8 kernel declines shapes it cannot pack (N % 32, K % 16, no split-K for K), so such a layer keeps Marlin (SM75) or the emulation (SM70) as before. This kernel comes first for every MXFP8 linear on SM70/SM75, not only for this checkpoint.
