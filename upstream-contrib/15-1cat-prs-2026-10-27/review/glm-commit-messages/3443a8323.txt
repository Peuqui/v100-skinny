fix(mxfp8): choose the kernel per layer shape; QPN8 declines what it cannot pack

Qpn8Mxfp8LinearKernel comes first for every MXFP8 linear on SM70 and
SM75, but the kernel choice saw no shape: a layer with N % 32 != 0, K %
16 != 0 or a K whose 16-element groups no split-K of the launch table
divides failed when its weights loaded, where Marlin (SM75) or the
emulation (SM70) would have served it. Mxfp8LinearLayerConfig now
carries the partition's (N, K), the ModelOpt, online and
compressed-tensors MXFP8 methods choose their kernel in create_weights,
and the QPN8 kernel declines those shapes through
fp8_qpn8_shape_reason, the check its weight preparation also uses. The
E8M0 exponent range (95..134, exact in QPN8's FP16 group scales) depends
on the weights and stays a load-time error naming the range.

Tests: tests/kernels/quantization/test_sm70_qpn8_mxfp8_linear.py, 24 passed on a
Tesla V100 and on a Quadro RTX 8000, each with stock Triton and with the sm7x
Triton build.
