perf(glm53): decode reads the expert scales as five-bit planes, no raster round trip

csf_32x2_v1 scales are re-encoded at load as base + 4-bit nibble + 1 high bit
per scale in the raster's positions (s5_scales.py): one block in six has a csf
exception record, which a kernel cannot look up in its loop, but outside layers
36 and 40 to 44 the scales of a row span at most 31, which five bits carry (the
next commit keeps the csf planes where a row spans more). The decode kernels
(m16n8k8 and m8n8k4) decode the planes in place; prefill and every other launch
get the raster of the routed experts written from them. Real GLM-5.3 layer 10,
decode of 8 tokens, ms per layer W13 + W2: RTX 8000 1.512 (csf expansion +
raster) -> 1.203, V100 1.166 -> 1.034; outputs bit-identical to the raster
kernels.
