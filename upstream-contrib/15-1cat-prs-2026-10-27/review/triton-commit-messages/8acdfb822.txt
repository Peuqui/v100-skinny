perf(mhc): prefill mHC projection on tensor cores for the sm7x route

The mHC pre stage projects every token's 4 x 4096 residual onto 24 mix
logits in FP32. Above 16 tokens the SM70 Triton kernel gave each (token,
column, split) its own program and re-read x 24 times; the TileLang
kernel on Turing read the 786 KB weight tile once per token. Both took
~450 us per 508-token chunk where reading x takes ~25 us.

With kernel_config.triton_sm7x_tensor_cores the projection runs on FP16
tensor cores: x is FP16, so the products are exact once the FP32 weight
is split into FP16 hi + lo * 2^-11 (22 of 24 mantissa bits). Tensor
cores truncate their FP32 accumulation, so every 16-wide K block starts
from zero and block sums add round-to-nearest in two levels; 32 K splits
are summed in FP64 and rounded once, and the consumers see one split.
One tile for both architectures. DSv4 decoder layers decide the route
when they are built; decode (<= 16 tokens) keeps its kernels.

CUDA graphs, us per call (less is better), today's route -> new:
  projection    tokens     17     64    508   1024   2048   4096
  RTX 8000 (TileLang)    28.9   74.7  428.6  460.4  877.4 1723.5
                     ->  26.8   30.8   78.0  145.6  263.8  506.7
  V100 (SM70 Triton)     21.3   55.0  467.5 1012.7 1988.5 3969.3
                     ->  17.7   24.5   64.1  113.0  201.8  378.2
  whole fused post+pre op, 508 tokens: RTX 546.3 -> 197.9,
  V100 597.4 -> 188.8; 2048 tokens: RTX 1336.4 -> 718.8,
  V100 2510.0 -> 716.0.
The DSv4 150k-prefill profile spends 2.8 s per RTX stage and 2.1 s per
V100 stage in this projection (20 and 16 calls per chunk).

Error of the logits against fp64, max |err| / sum|x*w| over 20 inputs
with the layer-10 hc_attn_fn of the checkpoint (508 tokens, consumer
split order): RTX 1.26e-8 -> 1.01e-8 (new lower on 20 of 20 inputs),
V100 1.09e-8 -> 1.05e-8 (16 of 20), mean RTX 1.69e-9 -> 1.26e-9, V100
1.65e-9 -> 1.48e-9. Fused op against today's route: residual equal,
post/comb mixes within 3.2e-6 relative, 0.004-0.016 % of the layer inputs
differ, by at most one FP16 step of their row.
